## 🤝 Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in **ArcadeVerse** and the **Nexus Spring Of Code – Winter Edition 2026** a harassment-free, welcoming, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community where every contributor — from first-time open-source students to seasoned web developers — feels empowered to learn, build, and succeed.

---

## 🌟 Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive, productive community environment include:

- **Demonstrating Empathy & Kindness:** Treating fellow contributors, reviewers, and maintainers with patience and mutual respect.
- **Welcoming Beginners:** Encouraging students making their first pull request, answering questions patiently, and providing constructive guidance.
- **Respecting Diverse Perspectives:** Being respectful of differing viewpoints, architectural opinions, and creative game designs.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code review feedback.
- **Graceful Accountability:** Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- **Code Integrity & Originality:** Striving for genuine learning, writing original vanilla JavaScript, and properly attributing any open-source algorithms or design inspirations.
- **Community First:** Focusing on what is best not just for individual points or rankings, but for the long-term health and quality of the ArcadeVerse project.

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment & Intimidation:** The use of sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention or advances, trolling, stalking, or harassing comments.
- **Offensive & Derogatory Language:** Insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, inflammatory remarks, or political insults in issue threads, commit messages, or pull requests.
- **Doxxing & Privacy Violations:** Publishing others' private information (such as physical or email addresses) without their explicit permission.
- **Plagiarism & Code Theft:** Submitting other people's games, copying proprietary code, or lifting projects from other repositories without proper open-source licensing and attribution.
- **Spam & Broken AI Dumps:** Submitting auto-generated, unverified AI code dumps that do not run locally or follow the repository's strict vanilla web standards.
- **Manipulative Activity & Cheating:** Creating duplicate accounts, submitting artificially split micro-PRs to inflate contribution numbers, or attempting to game the leaderboard scoring engine.
- **Unprofessional Demands:** Demanding point increases or level upgrades (`level1` → `level3`) from project admins after submitting small changes.
- **Other Unethical Conduct:** Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source environment.

## ⚖️ Enforcement Responsibilities & Scope

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and pull requests that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub issues, Pull Requests, discussions, code reviews), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or program in public spaces.

---

## 🚨 Enforcement Guidelines & Consequences

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action deemed in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

- **Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.
- **Consequence:** A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

- **Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions (e.g. submitting broken AI spam, opening duplicate unassigned PRs, or arguing aggressively in code reviews).
- **Consequence:** A formal written warning with consequences for continued behavior. Points earned from the offending contribution may be normalized or deducted.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

- **Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained disruptive behavior, repeated plagiarism, or persistent disrespect toward maintainers.
- **Consequence:** A temporary ban from opening issues, PRs, or participating in repository discussions for a specified period of time.

### 4. Permanent Ban & Event Disqualification

- **Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation, including sustained harassment of an individual, malicious code submission (cryptominers, malware, hidden tracking scripts), intentional cheating, or blatant intellectual property theft.
- **Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the ArcadeVerse project. The incident will be officially referred to the **Nexus Spring Of Code – Winter Edition 2026** organizing committee for **immediate disqualification from the program**.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the Project Lead & Admin:

- **Maintainer:** [Debasmita Bose (@DebasmitaBose0)](https://github.com/DebasmitaBose0)

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
